Paragraph Alignments

Left This paragraph is aligned to the left, which is the default alignment for most text content on the web.

Right This paragraph is right-aligned, often used for dates or signatures.

Center This paragraph is centered, commonly used for headings and titles.

Justify This paragraph is justified. The text stretches so that each line has equal width, creating a clean block appearance similar to newspapers and books.
